WHY I SERVE

I believe every person deserves an opportunity to hear the Gospel, encounter Jesus, and be equipped to help others follow Him.

My work is about more than one conversation. It is about building relationships, developing leaders, and seeing the Gospel take root in communities.

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Called to connect.
Committed to equip.

A personal ministry journey shaped by faith, relationships, and a desire to see lives transformed by Jesus.

I serve with The LIFE Ministry Tanzania (LMT), a Cru ministry helping people discover Jesus and equipping believers to become confident, multiplying disciples—so that everyone knows someone who truly follows Jesus.

Through personal evangelism, church and campus engagement, discipleship, and practical training, I work alongside others to make the Gospel known, strengthen local ministry, and equip believers to live out their faith.

I also partner with churches to fulfil the Great Commission by winning people to Christ, building them up in faith, and sending them out as Christ-centred, multiplying disciples who help others follow Jesus.
